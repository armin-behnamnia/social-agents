RL Persuader — Results & Diagnostics

Two PPO campaigns, one statistically firm finding, and the reward-channel defect that explains the ceiling.

CAMP-1 ppo/CAMP-2 ppo5p_rn/PPO • GPU50 paired seeds74 tests passing

Across two campaigns the learned persuader beats uniform-random strategy selection by a statistically firm margin (+5.8%, t=4.81) but only ties a hand-tuned constant (the heuristic). This report puts the metrics on one page, shows where learning stalled, and traces the ceiling to a saturated LLM reward channel that leaves the action nearly irrelevant to the reward.

Where we stand +

Campaign 2 delivers the first statistically firm positive result — but it is a tie with a constant, not a demonstration of learning.

Campaign 2 (ppo5p_rn/live_run1) ran 300 PPO iterations with five shared-policy persuaders on a 50-agent network. The formal comparison is a paired evaluation over 50 shared seeds: the learned greedy policy scores 10.625 against random's 10.038 (+5.8%, t=4.81, winning 39/50) and the heuristic's 10.520 (+1.0%, t=0.69).

The strategy signal is real — a fixed mid-strategy heuristic beats random too — so PPO captured something. But it captured a good constant, not the best one, and it never learned to exceed it.

reading the numbers

Paired, not unpaired. The auto-written results.json used independent seeds and 10 episodes (σ≈1.5) and even reported the opposite sign; it is superseded. The paired design removes between-seed variance — that is what turned t=0.69 into t=4.81.

Absolute rewards are not comparable across runs. The persuasiveness gate damps diffusion globally, so each run must be judged against its own baselines.

The paired verdict +

On shared seeds, the learned policy beats random on 39 of 50 episodes and the heuristic on 30 — the second is a coin flip.

why paired matters

Per-episode σ ≈ 1.5. Independent 10-episode evals have SE ≈ 0.47 — larger than the effect. Sharing seeds cancels the environment variance and leaves only the policy difference, shrinking the SE to 0.12.

The heuristic is a hard bar. It is one fixed strategy — index 90 = pathos / empathetic / evidence / measured — near the top of the reachable strategy space, so "beat it" means finding the best of 180 arms through noise. Its exact rendering appears in the message table below.

Training dynamics +

The training reward is flat; any apparent progress lives only in the noisier greedy-eval series.

Episode reward per iteration (5-iter samples) and greedy eval reward (every 20 iters). Fitted training slope +0.001/iter.
flat where it counts

No within-run learning. The training-reward slope is +0.001/iter over 300 iters — indistinguishable from zero. The eval series rises early (first-3 mean 9.20 → last-3 10.78) but with per-eval SE ≈ 0.17 that is ~1–2 SE.

The critic fits noise. Value loss falls steadily while reward does not — a sign the critic is absorbing episode variance, not signal.

Behavioural collapse +

Over 1200 trained actions the policy uses just three strategies (70% / 15% / 15%); a single 50-action replay showed only one. Random used 43 — strategy-level collapse, not targeting collapse.

the converged mode

Three arms, always. Across 24 episodes the policy picked #141 (ethos / urgent / social_proof) 836 times, #153 (ethos / empathetic / personal_story) 184, and #13 (logos / neutral / authority) 180 — a tiny fixed set, not a policy that keeps adapting.

This is the expected symptom. If the reward barely moves with the action (next section), the optimal policy is constant, and PPO finds one good constant then stops.

The reward channel is saturated +

The frozen LLM judge collapses 180 distinct messages onto 4–6 scores; 54% of all 2,160 judgments land on a single value, 0.9.

Distribution of judge persuasiveness over 180 strategies × 12 personas (n=2,160), read from the cached render+judge matrix.
PersonaJudgedDistinct scoresSDModal scoreModal share
why this caps everything

The multiplier is the only shaped path. p = [0.25·content_sim + C] · score. With score sd ≈ 0.05–0.07 and a 54% modal pile-up, changing strategy barely changes p — so the reward gradient is ≈ 0 for most arms.

Fix order matters. Reward reshaping and variance reduction (Rao-Blackwellizing the accept gate, more rollouts) cannot manufacture signal. The channel must first be made discriminative — e.g. a pairwise / ranking judge instead of absolute 0–10 scoring.

acceptance test for a repaired channel

Over 180 strategies for a fixed persona, the judge-score distribution should have SD ≳ 0.15 and no single bucket above ~40%. Today it is SD 0.072 and 54% on one bucket.

the generator end: variety without structure

The judge is only one end of the pipe. Does the strategy even change the post? We embedded all 180 rendered messages for a fixed persona with the same model the env uses (jina-v5) and measured how much of the message variation the strategy axes actually organise.

Nearest-neighbour purity of the message embedding for each strategy dimension, against chance (1 / number of levels). Above chance = the dimension reliably shapes the post.
variety ≠ learnable variety

The posts do differ. Mean pairwise cosine across the 180 messages is 0.31 — well short of identical (unrelated sentences score 0.09), and no pair exceeds 0.90.

But not by the strategy. Same-level and cross-level messages are nearly as similar (gap ≤ 0.04); tone, emphasis and intensity sit at or near chance for predicting a message's neighbours. Changing the label changes the words, but not in a consistent, generalisable direction — so the 180 arms look idiosyncratic to the agent.

Strategy reward landscape +

Across all 180 strategies the network reward spans –, but the middle half covers only 0.90 and the top decile just 0.61. The heuristic ranks 6th of 180; the learned policy's three modes rank 5th, 13th and 73rd.

Mean episode reward per strategy (all five persuaders fixed to that strategy), 6 paired seeds. Heuristic (90) and the three learned modes (141 / 153 / 13) highlighted against the best arm (168).
a plateau, not a gradient

Broad plateau. The middle half of the arms sits inside 0.90 reward points against a per-episode σ ≈ 1.5, so the reward gives PPO almost no signal to rank one good arm above another.

Only the tail is distinguishable. The reward sharply punishes a few bad arms (rank 170+ fall to ≈ 7.7) — enough to learn "avoid the bad", never to prefer the best.

The policy found the plateau. Its three modes rank 5, 13 and 73; the last one drags the mixture back to the heuristic's level (rank 6).

the channel sets the reward

Reward tracks the saturated score. Correlation between a strategy's mean judge score and its mean network reward is r = –. Because the judge collapses most strategies onto one value, the network reward inherits that flatness.

Order of operations. No optimizer or budget change can create spread the reward does not have — repair the channel first, then variance reduction pays off.

one-line verdict

PPO learned to avoid bad strategies and settle on a good one (+5.8% over random, significant) but the reward channel is too flat to reward anything finer — so it ties the best constant. Repair the channel first; variance fixes are necessary but not sufficient.

Appendix: a strategy definition that can carry learnable variation +

A better prompt on the same abstract grid is not the fix. Three separate properties are required, and a pilot of a redesigned action shows the generator can supply two of them — the third lives in the reward channel.

We tested a redesigned action — angle (which concern to foreground: cost / safety / fairness / freedom / community / effectiveness) × evidence (statistic / expert / anecdote / peers) × address (taxpayer / parent / worker) — rendered for 12 personas and judged, then scored against the three properties that separate “real variety” from “learnable variety”.

PropertyQuestionCurrent gridRedesigned pilotTargetVerdict
what the pilot proves

Realizable. Concrete slots are adopted by the generator — angle reaches 0.85 nearest-neighbour purity (chance 0.17) and address 0.77 (chance 0.33), where the old emphasis sat at chance (0.31). The posts genuinely differ.

Readable — improved, not solved. The judge spread widens (SD 0.062 → 0.095) and the modal bucket falls (71% → 57%), but stops short of the resolution RL needs.

Not yet conditional. The action has a large main effect (some angles are simply better for everyone) but almost no interaction with the target: the oracle gain from choosing the angle per persona is 0.012, and the persona × angle interaction is 4.5%. That is the same trap as the old grid — a constant best arm, so nothing target-specific to learn.

the proposed definition

1 · Action = a target-matched plan. Keep the slots that proved realizable (angle, address) and make the rest a checkable constraint rather than a vague adjective: the post must contain a digit (statistic), a named source (expert), a first-person detail (anecdote). Drop tone/intensity — they were adopted at chance and only cost persuasiveness.

2 · Readout must score the match. Conditionality cannot come from the generator alone. Replace the absolute 0–10 judge with a pairwise / Bradley–Terry judge over (post, target) pairs — “which of these two fits this reader?” — so the reward depends on the pairing, not on a global quality constant.

3 · Or select from a validated bank. The robust alternative: pre-render a bank of candidates per (target-class × angle × address), score them once, and let the policy select. Then the action→reward map is discrete, measured, and target-conditioned by construction — no reliance on the LLM obeying a label.

the readout half, measured: a Bradley–Terry judge

Fix #2 above is now implemented and measured. A pairwise judge plays 3,600 (post, target) arms off in head-to-head comparisons and fits a Bradley–Terry score to each; we then read the resulting reward through the same 50-agent network. The readout's resolution nearly triples, and the flat plateau becomes a gradient the optimizer can climb.

what the policy learned

It converges on evidence=anecdote and address=worker, cycling the angle between effectiveness (#68) and fairness (#32) — the same top region the independent landscape probe ranked. Final strategy entropy 0.39: a handful of arms, not one.

the caveat that frames the next step

The judge saturates near its ceiling (~0.99, 90–98% in the top bucket). The gain is real against this readout, but the readout scores persona-fit, not the message's direction toward the goal — so the next lever is measuring goal direction, not further tuning the optimizer.

campaign 4: the dynamics constants, measured

The influence weights α (four × 0.25) and the stance step β (0.4) were inherited placeholders — the paper writes p_ij ← InfluenceProbability(…) and Δs_j ← StateTransition(…) without expanding either. Two reproducible scripts replace the assumption with evidence.

α probe (Track A1)valuereading
Joint (β, κ) sweep against the paper's dynamics. β and the α-scale κ enter only through ρ = β·κ. Early ΔS and the plateau are jointly matchable near ρ ≈ 2, but late ΔS is structurally 0 — the transition reaches a static fixed point, not the paper's sustained ≈ −0.107.
why the α fit is a negative result

The design matrix has effective rank —/4 (profile- and degree-similarity are near-constant), so the four α's are not separately identifiable from behavior. A logistic fit returns values far from 0.25 while the design is collinear — noise. The equal weights are kept, now documented as an unidentifiable prior.

β is not the binding knob

No setting reproduces the paper's smooth late phase: the model either stays shallow or collapses to S = −1, with no stable intermediate consensus. The residual is the text-independent pull-to-source — handed to campaign 5, not fixed by re-tuning β.

campaign 5: goal-direction injection

The reward and the stance delta were both text-direction-blind: an off-goal-but-persuasive post was scored like an on-goal one, and the transition pulled the target toward the source regardless of what the post argued. validation gate (run before committing)

Any candidate strategy space must clear all three, measured exactly as here: realisability NN ≥ 0.60; readability judge SD ≥ 0.15 and modal < 40%; and a target×action oracle gain that exceeds the per-episode reward noise (SE ≈ 0.53). A space that fails the third is unlearnable no matter how diverse its posts.
